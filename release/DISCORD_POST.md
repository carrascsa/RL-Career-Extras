**RL Career Extras** is a free add-on for RL Career Hub that adds new career systems, a world-rankings hub and a live match caster. It runs alongside your install without modifying any game files. 🧪 **Playtest build: testers wanted!**
🏛️ **Club & Career**
* Hall of Fame: club history, records, legends, 19 achievements
* Academy: youth intakes, training, B team with its own Development League
* Club Life: scouting, game plans (right plan = weaker rival bot in real matches), press, dressing room, investors, sponsors, player talks, fatigue
🏟️ **Circuit & World Tour**
* S–D tier tournaments in 3v3, 2v2 and 1v1, plus freestyle and fun-mode cups
* Season awards, All-Star, Majors/Worlds rotating through 58 host cities with travel & jet lag
🌐 **RL World**
* All-time & current world rankings from real RLCS titles (2016–2026)
* New prospects every season, controversies, Y beefs, org buyouts & mergers
* Mod manager: turn any feature on/off per career
📺 **Caster**
* Replay your series with commentary
* **Live caster** for real Rocket League matches: original voices, 550 player names, reactive arena crowd
📥 **Install**
1. Download `RL-Career-Extras-0.1.0-beta.zip` from GitHub Releases: https://github.com/carrascsa/RL-Career-Extras/releases and extract it
2. Run `INSTALL.cmd` (finds RL Career Hub automatically)
3. Play from the new **RL Career Extras** desktop shortcut
Uninstall: `UNINSTALL.cmd`. Add-on data is stored in your save and ignored by the Hub without it, so **back up `%APPDATA%\rl-career-hub\saves` first**.
**Compatibility:** ✅ RL Career+ (loads automatically if installed) · ⚠️ other add-ons untested
